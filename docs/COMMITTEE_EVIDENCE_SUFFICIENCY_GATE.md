# Committee evidence sufficiency gate

The sufficiency gate checks conservative minimums for:
- total rows,
- official rows,
- outcome links,
- baseline references,
- NoTrade counterfactuals,
- RiskDenied counterfactuals,
- row-level coverage,
- summary-derived ratio,
- research-only and fixture-only ratios,
- no-lookahead safety.

What passing means:
- the research bundle is complete enough for committee benchmark analysis.

What passing does **not** mean:
- profitability proven,
- six-person activation approved,
- broker/live/runtime-LLM/Mamba readiness,
- real-money readiness.
