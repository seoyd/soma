# Eighteen Archetype Paper Rotation

The Sprint 102 bundle starts with a scenario pack, then builds a market context set, routes scenarios into short-term, long-term, crypto, and common-risk groups, selects members, emits paper-only proposals, adds paper-only entry timing, triggers debate when needed, runs a debate session, and hands the result to Risk Governor.

The member-selection stage keeps diagnostic members excluded by default and makes watchlist usage explicit. Proposal and entry-timing artifacts remain paper-only research output. Debate trigger/session are governance traces, not live AI debate. Risk Governor handoff remains the final veto.
