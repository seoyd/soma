# Retrospective Shadow replay policy

Historical source-bound replay proves protocol behavior and deterministic
source attribution only. It does not prove live prediction quality, trading
value, or historical real-time foresight.

Primary Momentum and Cycle/Risk opinions are created independently from exact
immutable results and sealed before any cross-agent relationship. V1 remains
advisory-only: it cannot reach the Chair, vote, receive reward or penalty,
change speaking rights, promote, or execute.
