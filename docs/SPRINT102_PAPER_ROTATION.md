# Sprint 102 Paper Rotation

Sprint 102 extends Sprint 101's committee-owned investor-archetype layer instead of introducing a new live framework. It adds a paper-only dry-run rotation across scenario packs, group routing, proposal generation, debate, chairman synthesis, and Risk Governor handoff.

This is appropriate now because Sprint 101 already built the archetype registry, roster plan, source-confidence weighting, and control-tower safety framing. Sprint 102 only exercises those artifacts in research mode.

This is not live agent activation. No central AI core, runtime implementation, training, live inference, live trading, broker/order/account path, or 18-live-agent activation is added.

Full workspace acceptance remains separate. Focused Sprint 102 checks can validate the paper bundle, but they cannot claim a full `cargo test --workspace --quiet` pass by themselves.
