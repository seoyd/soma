# Group Debate And Cross-Style Conflict

Sprint 102 records cross-group debates when timing, risk, or style conflicts surface. The conflict matrix is used to make short-term vs long-term, crypto vs equity, macro vs liquidity, and opportunity-cost vs risk-veto disagreements explicit.

Resolutions stay conservative: NoTrade, RiskDeny, or NeedMoreEvidence remain valid outcomes, and Risk Governor keeps the final veto.
