# Chairman Rulebook Safety Repair

Sprint 100 repairs unsafe chairman rules through a paper-only governance flow:

- unsafe rule closure records the blocked or repaired rule risk,
- the V2 draft captures the repaired paper-only rulebook text,
- Risk Governor review confirms the chairman still cannot bypass final veto,
- owner review documents human oversight before any paper-use recommendation,
- paper-only approval means the draft is suitable only for research dry-run governance,
- no live rule mutation is added anywhere in the process.
