# Six-persona design readiness

Sprint 33 adds a **design-review-only** gate for whether it is even reasonable to discuss a 6-person committee.

## What it checks

- enough official scenarios
- enough total scenarios
- disagreement is present but controlled
- groupthink is not too high
- risk overblocking is not too high
- research-only ratio is bounded

## What it does not do

- it does not activate 6 personas
- it does not change the current Trinity runtime
- it does not recommend live trading or real-money use

Fixture-only or research-only evidence cannot pass this gate.
