# Owner Control Tower Interaction

Sprint 53 extends Soma Control Tower with an owner interaction layer.

## Owner panel

The dashboard now shows:
- review queue summary,
- pending review items,
- recent owner inputs,
- active thesis notes,
- blocked owner inputs,
- paper-confirmed items,
- reanalysis requests,
- owner policy warnings.

## Candidate panel updates

Candidates now expose:
- owner feedback history,
- hold/dismiss/reanalysis flags,
- paper-confirm state,
- linked thesis notes.

## Human confirm panel updates

Human confirm items now show:
- allowed owner actions,
- forbidden owner actions,
- whether `PaperConfirm` is allowed,
- why `PaperConfirm` is allowed or forbidden.

## Hard safety limits

There are still:
- no order buttons,
- no broker controls,
- no account controls,
- no live execution controls.
