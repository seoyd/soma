# Debate Evidence Closure

Sprint 100 debate closure turns prior NeedMoreEvidence warnings into explicit paper-only follow-up records:

- NeedMoreEvidence closure shows which missing items were resolved and which still require caution.
- The evidence gap plan lists the remaining paper research needed before a dry-run recommendation.
- Dissent coverage checks that minority objections are recorded instead of silently compressed away.
- Participation balance checks that debate members contribute without one voice dominating the session.
- Groupthink risk stays visible by tying balance and dissent tracking to a paper-only warning narrative.
