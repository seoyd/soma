# Sprint 99 Committee Quality Hardening

Sprint 99 follows Sprint 98 because architecture correction had to come before quality hardening. Sprint 98 established the committee-owned AI core model; Sprint 99 measures proposal quality, timing quality, debate quality, rulebook quality, scorecard calibration, replay traceability, and workspace-truth reporting on top of that corrected architecture.

This sprint is not live agent activation, not runtime inference, not training, not broker integration, and not live trading. It remains research-only, paper-only, local-only.

Full workspace acceptance remains separate. Focused Sprint 99 tests and reports do not claim `cargo test --workspace --quiet` success.
