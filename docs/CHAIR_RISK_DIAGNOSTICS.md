# Chair and risk diagnostics

Sprint 33 adds explanation layers without changing execution decisions.

## Chair diagnostics

- speaker traces
- selected vs filtered speakers
- cluster penalty visibility
- contrarian inclusion visibility
- disagreement and groupthink status

## Risk bridge diagnostics

- committee candidate summary
- derived risk proposal summary
- risk denial reason-code mapping
- emergency stop / cooldown / quality / edge blocks

These reports are explanatory only. They do **not** weaken the Risk Governor.
